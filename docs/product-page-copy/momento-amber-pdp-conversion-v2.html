Good to know

A few questions, simply answered.

Only the details that may matter before you choose Momento.

Do I need an app or subscription?

No. Momento opens in your phone’s browser, so there is no app to download. No subscription is required for the included 365-memory capacity. An internet connection is required.

Can someone see my Journal just by touching it?

No. Touching or scanning leads to the PIN screen on an unauthorised device. Your memories stay behind the six-digit Owner PIN you create when you first open Momento.

Can I keep a memory from an earlier date?

Yes. Choose today or return to a past date, then create or edit its Daily Memory Stamp when you are ready.

What does “365” mean?

One Momento Journal can hold up to 365 dated Daily Memory Stamps. They do not need to be consecutive, and Momento does not ask you to record every day.

Will it work with my phone, and does the Journal need charging?

The Journal itself needs no battery or charging. Use touch access on a supported NFC phone, or scan the included QR card to open the same Momento space in your mobile browser.
