## How Momento works

### 1. Write and collect

Journal the feeling, add a receipt or note, and keep the tactile pieces of the day inside your paper pages and fabric folio.

### 2. Tap your Journal

Hold a compatible NFC-enabled phone near the built-in entry point to open your Journal. No battery, Bluetooth pairing, or app download is required. A QR/recovery card is also included.

### 3. Make a Daily Memory Stamp

Choose the date—including a date in the past—and add up to nine photographs. Your private memory space is protected by the six-digit Owner PIN you create during activation.

### 4. Return to the whole day

Open the Journal again whenever you want to see the page and the scenes together. You can also save or share an individual Daily Memory Stamp as an image.

## The physical Journal

### Handmade from the first cut.

Each Journal begins as a full piece of leather. It is individually cut, assembled, and finished by hand, with a fabric folio stitched for the design. The folio adds an interior pocket and zippered pouch for the loose fragments that make a journal feel lived in: tickets, notes, photographs, stickers, and small paper keepsakes.

Three elastic bands hold three replaceable inserts, supplied in a mix of dotted, grid, lined, and blank pages. When one chapter fills, replace the paper and keep the cover.

Because each Journal is handmade and leather is a natural material, small variations in grain, tone, and finish are part of its character. No two will be perfectly identical.

## FAQ

### Do I need to download an app?

No. Momento opens in your phone’s web browser. An internet connection is required.

### How do I open the digital Journal?

Tap the built-in NFC entry point with a compatible NFC-enabled smartphone. Your package also includes a QR/recovery card, which provides another way to reach your Journal.

### Does the NFC chip store my photographs?

No. The passive NFC chip opens the dedicated digital Journal connected to your physical Journal. Your photographs are stored in private cloud storage, not on the chip.

### Does the chip need charging?

No. The NFC entry point is passive and requires no battery or Bluetooth pairing.

### How private is my Journal?

Your digital memory space is private by default and protected by the six-digit Owner PIN you create during activation. Avoid sharing your PIN or recovery information with anyone you do not want to access the Journal.

### Is there a subscription?

No subscription is required for the storage capacity included with your Momento Journal.

### How many photographs can I add?

You can add up to nine photographs to each Daily Memory Stamp, across up to 365 dated Stamps—a total included capacity of up to 3,285 photographs.

### Can I add a memory from an earlier date?

Yes. You can choose a past date and create its Daily Memory Stamp later.

### Can I save or share my memories?

You can save or share an individual Daily Memory Stamp as an image. Full-Journal export is not currently included.

### Can I store videos or voice recordings?

Momento currently supports photographs in the Daily Memory Stamp experience.

### What happens if I change phones?

Your memories are connected to the Journal’s dedicated digital space, not stored on one phone. Use the Journal entry point or the included QR/recovery card and your Owner PIN to return to it.

### What paper comes with the Journal?

Three replaceable, 30-page inserts are included in a curated mix of dotted, grid, lined, and blank pages. They match the Journal’s size and can later be replaced with standard refills of the same size.

### Can I make my Journal bulkier?

Yes. If you prefer a fuller setup, leave a note with your order and we will include additional elastic bands at no extra charge.

### Can you personalize it?

Yes. Complimentary name embossing is included. Enter the name and choose gold, silver, or blind embossing in the order note at checkout. English letters and a heart symbol are available; other special characters are not supported.

### Will mine look exactly like the photographs?

Your selected design includes the leather color, fabric, hardware, and decorative charms shown. Because every Journal is handmade and leather is a natural material, slight variations in grain, tone, and finish make each piece unique.

### How should I care for the leather?

A leather care guide is included with your Journal. Natural leather will soften and develop character with use; follow the included instructions for ongoing care.

### What is the difference between Momento Edition and Classic Edition?

Both editions include the same handmade physical Journal experience. Momento Edition adds the built-in NFC entry point, private PIN-protected digital memory space, and QR/recovery card. Classic Edition is the physical Journal without the digital layer.
