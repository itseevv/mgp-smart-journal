P2 · Privacy and sharing

Private access, intentional sharing

Your Journal does not open itself to the room.

Touch and QR are entrances, not storage. Your photographs, words, and Voice Notes live behind the Owner PIN in the Journal’s private digital space.

Owner PIN

Touching alone does not reveal your memories.

An unauthorised device reaches the PIN screen. An unlocked device can view, create, edit, delete, and export, so the Owner PIN should remain private.

  • No public Journal page
  • No visitor or read-only PIN
  • Inactive devices relock after about one hour
  • Recovery Passcode can reset a forgotten PIN
Anyone given the Owner PIN receives full Journal access, not view-only access.

Save or Share

Share one Stamp, not the whole Journal.

Export a Daily Memory Stamp as a 1080 × 1920 PNG. The image can contain its date, photographs, One line to keep, and additional words.

  • Voice Note stays inside the unlocked Journal
  • No Owner PIN, QR, or Recovery Passcode appears in the image

  • Customer content is not sold, used for ads, or used to train AI models

P3 · Detailed FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything else, when you need it.

Purchase-critical answers appear near the top. Lower-frequency limits and recovery details stay here.

Do I need an app or subscription?

No app is required. Momento opens in your phone’s browser. No subscription is required for the included capacity. Internet access is required.

Can anyone see my Journal by touching it?

No. An unauthorised device sees the PIN screen. A person who knows the Owner PIN receives full access, so do not share it with anyone you do not want to view or change the Journal.

Can I record a day from the past?

Yes. Choose “Seal Another Day” and select a past date. Future dates are not available, and each local calendar date can hold one Daily Memory Stamp.

What happens when I reach 365 Stamps?

Your existing Stamps remain available to view and edit. A 366th Stamp cannot be created. Deleting an existing Stamp releases one place, but the deleted content cannot be recovered.

Can I edit or delete a saved Stamp?

Yes. You can edit its date, line, additional words, photographs, order, Cover Scrap, and Voice Note. Deletion is permanent and currently has no recovery bin.

What exactly can I share?

You can save or share one Stamp as a 1080 × 1920 PNG containing its visual and written content. Voice Note audio, public links, and full-Journal export are not currently included.

What if I change or lose my phone?

On a new phone, touch or scan and enter your Owner PIN. An inactive lost phone relocks after about one hour. Reset the PIN with the Recovery Passcode if you are concerned the old device or PIN may be compromised.

What if I lose the Recovery Card?

You can continue using the Journal through touch access while you still know the Owner PIN. Contact support for the next recovery step if the card is lost and you later forget the PIN.

Does the Journal need charging?

No. The built-in entry is passive and needs no battery, charging, or Bluetooth pairing. Your phone must have power and an internet connection.

Can a Classic Journal be upgraded later?

A Classic-to-Momento upgrade is planned but is not currently open. Timing, pricing, and the upgrade method have not yet been announced.
