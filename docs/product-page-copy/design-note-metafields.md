# Classic Edition — Design Note Metafields

Use `Heading` as the section title and `Body` as the single-paragraph excerpt. The visible section eyebrow is `DESIGN NOTE` for every product.

## Amber

**Heading:** Where the afternoon turns golden.

**Body:** Amber carries the warmth of worn tan leather, sunlit paper, and keepsakes gathered far from home. Its faded garden of rust, muted rose, blue-gray, and green is finished with a glint of warm metal—something already storied, familiar from the very first touch.

## Royale

**Heading:** An heirloom in a quieter key.

**Body:** Deep brown leather gives Royale the gravity of old mahogany, while ivory-and-gold flowers shimmer softly like silk in candlelight. Richness appears slowly—in texture, shadow, and small flashes of gold—giving it the quiet intimacy of an heirloom made entirely personal.

## Bloom

**Heading:** Everything is coming into flower.

**Body:** Bloom arrives in saturated pink, coral, blush, green, and blue—a garden at the moment it refuses to be contained. Petals overlap and colors wander with the bright abundance of flowers gathered in both arms: joyful, expressive, and a little extravagant.

## Porcelain

**Heading:** Flowers remembered by candlelight.

**Body:** Porcelain is pale at first glance, then quietly shadowed. Ivory leather opens onto cream petals, faded rose, burgundy, and soft green, like an antique box holding a garden preserved at dusk—delicate, never fragile, and deepening in mystery with time.

## Blush

**Heading:** A love letter tied in black ribbon.

**Body:** Dusty rose leather, ink-drawn blossoms, and a black bow give Blush the intimacy of a love letter dressed for its own occasion. Sweetness meets a dark, precise line, carrying the mood of perfume on paper—gentle, poised, and quietly self-assured.

## Flame

**Heading:** Red, without apology.

**Body:** Flame has the presence of a red dress entering a quiet room. Scarlet leather frames ruby, coral, burgundy, and cream blooms, where deep petals smolder and pale ones catch the light—a lush romance made for chapters lived with appetite and courage.

## Eden

**Heading:** After the rain, the garden is still.

**Body:** Eden rests in the deep green of leaves after rain. White flowers open with pale blue shadow, blush, and a trace of gold, holding the hush of cool air, wet earth, and a path beneath the branches—lush, private, and still growing.

## Aurora

**Heading:** The sky before morning chooses a color.

**Body:** Aurora moves like color across water. Aqua leather meets a loose wash of pink, yellow, green, and blue, each shade drifting into the next like paint still wet on the brush—the lightness of early morning, bright with possibility.

## Eclipse

**Heading:** A garden written in moonlight.

**Body:** In Eclipse, white peonies rise from near-black leather as if the night itself had brought them into bloom. Deep green leaves disappear into shadow; tiny golden stars remain suspended between the petals.

## Midnight

**Heading:** A hidden garden after dark.

**Body:** Midnight opens like a secret room after dusk. Deep navy leather surrounds twining leaves, russet irises, pale birds, and shadow-blue foliage, while silver-toned charms and small crystals glimmer like objects from an old story—a garden that reveals itself slowly after dark.
