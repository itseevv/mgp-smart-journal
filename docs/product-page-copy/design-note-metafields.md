# Classic Edition — Design Note Metafields

Use `Heading` as the section title and `Body` as the supporting rich text. The visible section eyebrow is `DESIGN NOTE` for every product.

## Amber

**Heading:** Where the afternoon turns golden.

**Body:** Amber carries the warmth of worn leather, sunlit paper, and keepsakes gathered far from home. Across the tan cover, a faded garden of rust, muted rose, blue-gray, and green seems softened by years that have not happened yet.

A glint of warm metal catches among the flowers like a small treasure found at the bottom of a travel bag. It has the romance of something already storied—the quiet beauty of an object that feels familiar from the very first touch.

## Royale

**Heading:** An heirloom in a quieter key.

**Body:** Deep brown leather gives Royale the gravity of old mahogany, while ivory-and-gold flowers drift across its folio with the muted shimmer of silk in candlelight. There is something of an old library, a velvet-lined box, a room remembered by lamplight.

Its richness never asks for attention; it reveals itself slowly in texture, shadow, and small flashes of gold. Royale feels as though it has crossed generations to arrive here—formal enough to feel precious, intimate enough to become entirely personal.

## Bloom

**Heading:** Everything is coming into flower.

**Body:** Bloom arrives in a rush of saturated pink, coral, blush, green, and blue—a garden at the exact moment it refuses to be contained. Petals overlap, colors wander, and the cover holds the exuberance of flowers gathered with both arms.

There is no shyness in it, only joy: bright, abundant, a little extravagant. Bloom feels like the first warm evening of the year, a favorite dress worn for no occasion, and the delicious certainty that life is opening again.

## Porcelain

**Heading:** Flowers remembered by candlelight.

**Body:** Porcelain is pale at first glance, then quietly shadowed. Ivory leather opens onto a dark botanical world of cream petals, faded rose, burgundy, and soft green—like lifting the lid of an antique box and finding a garden preserved inside.

The flowers seem to emerge from dusk rather than sit upon it. Delicate, but never fragile, Porcelain holds the romance of old ceramics, pressed bouquets, and beautiful things whose mystery deepens with time.

## Blush

**Heading:** A love letter tied in black ribbon.

**Body:** Dusty rose leather, ink-drawn blossoms, and a black bow give Blush the intimacy of a letter dressed for its own occasion. Its sweetness is tempered by a dark, precise line—the small gesture that turns softness into poise.

Romantic without innocence, polished without distance, Blush carries the mood of perfume left on paper and ribbons kept long after the gift is gone. It is gentle, but it is never unsure of itself.

## Flame

**Heading:** Red, without apology.

**Body:** Flame has the presence of a red dress entering a quiet room. Scarlet leather frames a garden of ruby, coral, burgundy, and cream; the deeper flowers smolder at the edges while pale petals catch the last of the light.

Nothing here whispers. The colors are lush, the romance unmistakable, the feeling closer to velvet curtains and late-summer heat than to simple red. Flame belongs to chapters lived with appetite, courage, and a little beautiful excess.

## Eden

**Heading:** After the rain, the garden is still.

**Body:** Eden rests in the deep green of leaves after rain. White flowers open across the folio, touched by pale blue shadow, blush, and a trace of gold, as though the garden has gone quiet just long enough to be held.

Its abundance feels hushed rather than wild: cool air, wet earth, a path disappearing beneath the branches. Eden carries the rare calm of a secret place—lush, private, and always on the verge of growing.

## Aurora

**Heading:** The sky before morning chooses a color.

**Body:** Aurora moves like color across water. Aqua leather meets a loose wash of pink, yellow, green, and blue, each shade drifting into the next with the freedom of a half-remembered dream.

Nothing settles into a perfect flower or a fixed horizon. It holds the lightness of early morning, paint still wet on the brush, and the feeling of leaving with no map—bright, open, and full of possibility.

## Eclipse

**Heading:** A garden written in moonlight.

**Body:** In Eclipse, white peonies rise from near-black leather as if the night itself had brought them into bloom. Deep green leaves disappear into shadow; tiny golden stars remain suspended between the petals.

Pearl and silver-toned details catch the light like distant constellations. Dark, feminine, and quietly dramatic, Eclipse belongs to the hour when the world falls silent and every private thought becomes luminous.

## Midnight

**Heading:** A hidden garden after dark.

**Body:** Midnight opens like a secret room after dusk. Deep navy leather surrounds a tapestry of twining leaves, russet irises, pale birds, and shadow-blue foliage—a garden that reveals a little more each time the light moves across it.

A sculpted silver rose, a snowflake, butterflies, and small blue crystals glimmer like objects gathered from an old story. Midnight feels nocturnal and enchanted, touched by the peculiar beauty of things discovered when everyone else is asleep.
