# Momento PDP Information Architecture v1

> Status: framework for review
> Updated: 2026-07-22
> Source of truth: `momento-pdp-questions-final-review.md`
> Scope: page hierarchy, module responsibilities, and content ownership. This is not final storefront copy or a metafield schema.

## 1. Page role

Each of the ten Momento product listings uses one shared Momento product template. The page must do four jobs:

1. Sell the selected handmade Journal as a complete physical product.
2. Make the Momento digital memory layer understandable without positioning the product as an NFC device.
3. Resolve purchase-critical concerns about access, privacy, storage, compatibility, recovery, and ongoing cost.
4. Help the customer choose between Classic and Momento without confusing the two editions.

## 2. Communication hierarchy

Momento is journal-first. The customer-facing hierarchy is:

1. Momento Journal
2. Daily Memory Stamp
3. Month Sheet and Journal Home
4. Owner PIN and Recovery Card
5. Touch the Journal or scan the card
6. NFC as a secondary technical explanation

The page must downplay the term NFC without hiding the digital experience. Above the fold explains the outcome and the relationship between paper and digital memory. NFC appears only when explaining how touch access works, compatibility, or troubleshooting.

## 3. Information layers

### P0 — Purchase decision

Information needed to understand the offer and add the selected product to cart:

- selected design, product media, price, size, availability, and personalization;
- what is included physically and digitally;
- the core difference between Classic and Momento;
- no app and no subscription for included capacity;
- 365 dated Stamps, 1–9 photos per Stamp, optional text and Voice Note;
- primary add-to-cart action and delivery/returns links.

### P1 — Product understanding

Information needed to understand the experience:

- the relationship between the paper page and a Daily Memory Stamp;
- how to open the Journal and Seal a Day;
- what a Stamp contains;
- how Month Sheet works;
- how backdating and the 365 capacity work.

### P2 — Trust and control

Information needed to feel comfortable using the digital layer:

- Owner PIN and device access;
- what happens if another person touches the Journal;
- the absence of visitor/read-only access;
- Save or Share boundaries;
- Recovery Card and forgotten-PIN recovery;
- compatibility, internet requirements, device changes, and support;
- approved privacy and data-use promises.

### P3 — Detailed reassurance

Information for lower-frequency concerns:

- deletion and editing rules;
- the 365-limit state;
- unsupported media and export limitations;
- card loss, failed access, and support path;
- Classic upgrade status;
- detailed shipping, personalization, returns, and warranty links.

## 4. Recommended page sequence

### Module 1 — Product hero and purchase panel

**Purpose:** Establish the selected Journal, the Momento difference, and the purchase action without leading with technology.

**Customer questions:**

- Which Journal am I buying?
- What makes this Momento rather than Classic?
- What size, price, and personalization options are available?
- Is there an app or ongoing subscription?

**Required information:**

- product title and selected design;
- product media showing the physical Journal first;
- short Momento value explanation;
- price, size/variant, availability, quantity, personalization, and add-to-cart;
- compact facts: no app, no subscription for included capacity, 365 dated Stamps;
- shipping/returns links rather than long policy text.

**Do not include:** NFC in the H1 or primary value proposition; lifetime storage; unsupported privacy absolutes.

### Module 2 — What is included

**Purpose:** Make the complete package and Momento premium tangible.

**Required information:**

- Journal cover and design-specific fabric folio;
- three paper inserts and elastic bands;
- design-specific hardware and charms;
- Recovery Card;
- Journal pouch, leather care guide, gift packaging, and complimentary embossing;
- included digital Journal, 365 Stamp capacity, photo/text/Voice Note capability, and PNG sharing.

The physical contents appear before the digital contents to preserve the journal-first hierarchy.

### Module 3 — Quick purchase answers

**Purpose:** Borrow Memoried's strongest conversion pattern by answering high-risk questions immediately after the purchase block.

**Use five compact accordions or statements:**

1. How does Momento work?
2. Can someone see my Journal just by touching it?
3. Can I add a day from the past?
4. Do I need an app or subscription?
5. What if touch access does not work?

Answers stay brief here and link or scroll to the detailed sections below.

### Module 4 — The physical Journal and selected design

**Purpose:** Establish craftsmanship and design value before moving into the digital experience.

**Required information:**

- design-specific leather colour, fabric pattern, hardware, and charms;
- handmade construction and natural material variation;
- folio pockets and zippered pouch;
- paper insert mix and replaceability;
- selected size and closed dimensions;
- A6 pen loop / A5 no pen loop;
- design-specific editorial note.

This section varies by product listing even though the Momento experience is shared.

### Module 5 — One moment, two layers

**Purpose:** Explain the product model emotionally and visually before teaching interface details.

**Information structure:**

- the paper Journal holds handwriting and tactile keepsakes;
- the Daily Memory Stamp holds photographs, additional words, and a Voice Note;
- the two layers belong to the same dated memory;
- technology remains quiet until the customer chooses to open it.

This module should use a paired visual of a physical page and its corresponding Stamp.

### Module 6 — How to Seal a Day

**Purpose:** Show the complete everyday flow using customer actions rather than technical terminology.

**Steps:**

1. Write and collect in the physical Journal.
2. Touch the Journal or scan the Recovery Card.
3. Unlock with the Owner PIN when required.
4. Choose today or an earlier date.
5. Add the required line and Cover Scrap, then optional photos, text, and Voice Note.
6. Seal the day and return to it from Month Sheet.

First activation and PIN creation should be a short note, not a dominant step in the emotional sequence.

### Module 7 — Inside a Daily Memory Stamp

**Purpose:** Make the digital product concrete and remove uncertainty about what can be saved.

**Required information:**

- one date;
- one required “One line to keep,” up to 200 characters;
- one required Cover Scrap;
- up to eight additional photos;
- optional additional text;
- one optional Voice Note, recorded in the browser, up to five minutes;
- text and Voice Note can be used together;
- saved Stamps can be edited; deleted Stamps cannot currently be recovered.

Unsupported formats should not dominate this module. Video, locations, music, links, and uploaded audio belong in FAQ only if customers are likely to expect them.

### Module 8 — 365 days, used your way

**Purpose:** Explain capacity without turning Momento into a streak or productivity product.

**Required information:**

- up to 365 dated Daily Memory Stamps;
- dates do not need to be consecutive;
- customers can record today or a past date, but not a future date;
- one Stamp per local calendar date;
- Month Sheet displays only saved days and does not show missed-day pressure;
- up to nine photos per Stamp and up to 3,285 photos across a full Journal;
- at 365, existing Stamps remain viewable/editable; deleting one releases a place; no current expansion.

### Module 9 — Private access and ownership boundaries

**Purpose:** Answer who can open and control the Journal without overclaiming privacy.

**Required information:**

- content sits behind the six-digit Owner PIN;
- touching or scanning alone does not reveal Journal content;
- an unlocked device has full access, not read-only access;
- anyone given the Owner PIN receives view, create, edit, delete, and export permissions;
- there is no visitor mode or public/read-only Journal link;
- photos, text, and Voice Notes are stored in private cloud storage, not on the NFC chip;
- customer content is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models;
- necessary service providers or authorised support may process content under the privacy policy.

Do not use “100% private,” “100% secure,” “only you can ever access it,” or equivalent claims.

### Module 10 — Save or Share

**Purpose:** Separate sharing one memory from sharing access to the Journal.

**Required information:**

- a single Stamp can be saved or shared as a 1080 × 1920 PNG;
- the PNG can contain the Journal name, date, required line, additional text, and all photos;
- Voice Note is not included and no audio link is generated;
- sharing the image does not share the Journal, Owner PIN, QR, or Recovery Passcode;
- full-Journal export and public/read-only links are not currently included.

### Module 11 — Access, compatibility, and recovery

**Purpose:** Resolve practical use concerns in one place.

**Required information:**

- Momento runs in a current mobile browser and requires no app;
- Safari is recommended on iPhone and Chrome on Android;
- an internet connection is required;
- Voice Note recording requires microphone permission;
- touch access requires a supported/enabled NFC phone, but QR provides the same Journal functionality;
- the Journal entry requires no battery, charging, or Bluetooth pairing;
- a new phone can access the Journal using the entry point and Owner PIN;
- inactive devices relock after approximately one hour, and users can lock manually;
- Recovery Passcode resets a forgotten PIN and invalidates prior device access;
- lost-card and failed-entry cases direct customers to the approved support contact.

Do not claim compatibility with every phone or browser.

### Module 12 — Use cases and gifting

**Purpose:** Help the customer recognise when Momento fits their life.

**Recommended grouped scenarios:**

- everyday life and a year of growth;
- travel and major life chapters;
- weddings, anniversaries, new babies, graduations, moves, and new jobs;
- gifting to oneself or another person.

Use no more than three or four scenario groups on the PDP. Additional occasions can live in campaign or collection content.

### Module 13 — Classic versus Momento

**Purpose:** Help customers choose without making Classic feel incomplete.

**Comparison dimensions:**

- same core handmade Journal, folio, paper, and craftsmanship;
- Classic is the physical journaling experience;
- Momento adds built-in touch access, the digital Journal, Owner PIN, Recovery Card, Daily Memory Stamps, and included capacity;
- Classic-to-Momento upgrade is a confirmed future direction but is not currently available.

Until upgrade timing, price, and method are confirmed, the future upgrade should appear only as a qualified FAQ answer or note—not as a primary comparison benefit.

### Module 14 — Proof, FAQ, and final purchase action

**Purpose:** Close remaining objections and return the customer to purchase.

**Proof structure:**

- use Momento-specific reviews for digital-experience claims;
- Classic reviews may support physical craftsmanship only and must not imply review of Momento functions;
- show support, making, delivery, and returns links near the final CTA.

**FAQ topics:**

- app, network, battery, and phone access;
- previous dates and future dates;
- PIN, visitors, changing phones, forgotten PIN, and lost Recovery Card;
- subscription, 365 capacity, editing, deletion, and export;
- Voice Note sharing and unsupported media;
- Classic difference and future upgrade;
- personalization, paper refills, care, shipping, returns, and support.

The final CTA uses the currently selected product and variant. It should not send customers back to a separate generic Momento landing page.

## 5. Content ownership before field design

| Content group | Recommended owner | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Title, price, variants, inventory, product media, purchase form | Shopify native product data | Transactional and different for each listing |
| Design name, design story, leather/fabric/hardware description, design assets | Product-specific content | Different across the ten designs |
| Size, closed dimensions, pen-loop detail, included paper size | Product or variant-specific content | Changes by listing or size |
| Standard physical package structure | Shared Momento content with limited product overrides | Mostly identical, but design/size details vary |
| Digital package, Stamp anatomy, 365 rules, privacy, Save or Share, compatibility, recovery | Shared Momento content | Same across all ten listings and should be updated once |
| How Momento works and section-level educational media | Shared Momento content | Consistent product education |
| Classic versus Momento comparison | Shared edition-comparison content | Used across multiple products and pages |
| Shipping, returns, warranty, privacy, and support | Store policy or global setting | Must stay consistent outside the PDP |
| Module order, layout, labels, and component behaviour | Momento product template | Presentation logic, not product content |

## 6. Reuse rules for ten Momento listings

- All ten Momento listings use the same `product.momento` template.
- Shared Momento education and trust content should not be copied manually into ten product descriptions.
- Each listing supplies only its transactional data, design story, design assets, size/dimensions, and any package variation.
- The template renders shared Momento information from one maintained source.
- Product-specific content may override shared content only when a real physical or commercial difference exists.

## 7. Content that should stay out of the main PDP narrative

- database, hosting, encryption, internal support, or legal workflow details;
- NFC as the product identity;
- future features presented as currently available;
- lifetime or forever-storage claims until formally approved;
- full-Journal export, visitor mode, read-only sharing, offline use, or uploaded Voice Notes;
- all ten design stories on every product listing;
- unqualified security or exclusivity promises.

## 8. Decisions remaining before final copy

### Required for storefront completion

- closed dimensions for each A5/A6 design;
- approved cancellation/returns treatment for personalised products;
- final support email or contact-form URL.

### Product UX details that affect copy precision

- additional-text character and formatting limit;
- long-text behaviour in the PNG export.

### Optional claims or future features

- whether to publish any total Voice Note storage/file-size detail;
- Classic upgrade timing, method, and price;
- whether a formally defined lifetime-storage promise will ever be introduced.

## 9. Gate to the next stage

After this architecture is approved:

1. convert each content group into a Shopify ownership model;
2. define the minimal product metafields and shared metaobject definitions;
3. identify which existing Classic fields can be reused safely;
4. build one pilot Momento listing and assign `product.momento`;
5. verify the pilot before duplicating the structure across the remaining nine listings;
6. write final copy only against approved fields and confirmed product facts.
